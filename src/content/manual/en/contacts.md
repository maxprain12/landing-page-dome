In Pro, **People** is the **Contacts** hub: records with history, a next step, and the material that explains why you are writing. It does not replace a team CRM. It stops the follow-up from living in another tool while the PDF lives in Dome.

1. **Open Contacts.** In the Pro edition, the Contacts hub lives in the main navigation.
2. **Create or open a record.** Add the next step and relate the resource that explains the conversation.
3. **Ask Many for the follow-up.** With the person and material open, Many prepares the email or note.

![People and follow-up scene in Dome.](/how/how-people.png)

*Each person, next to the resource that makes the next step make sense.*

## What a record keeps

Name, role, notes, interactions, and the next step. You can link identities (email, social) after you connect a channel. Many can help create or update a record from a resource or a message, and you review the result.

> **Product names.** The UI says **Contacts**. Code docs talk about the People module. It is the same surface.

## From the record to the action

Open the person and the related PDF or note. Ask Many for the follow-up email. Sending does not happen in this step: continue with [email and social with approval](/en/manual/email-and-social). If you do not have a library yet, go back to [importing resources](/en/manual/library).
