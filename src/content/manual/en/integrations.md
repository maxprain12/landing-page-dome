Connect services and review the access you grant Dome.

## Requirements

- An account for the service you want to connect.
- Valid permissions and credentials for that service.

## Steps

1. For email, calendar, social and MCP, open the relevant Settings section. For GitHub, open its hub and connect your account.
2. Complete connection or configure the credentials requested by the integration.
3. Check account status and test a read before making changes.
4. Review recipients, content and scope before approving a write or publication action.

## Expected result

Integration tools become available according to the account, permissions and configuration.

## If something fails

If an account stops responding, check its status and authenticate again when requested. An MCP server may need a runtime, allowed paths or its own variables.
