Turn documents into study material and review what you learned.

## Requirements

- Resources with accessible text.
- Configured Many to generate material.

## Steps

1. Open Learn and start creating study material.
2. Choose material type, sources and configuration.
3. Review generated questions, answers and references before studying.
4. Open a flashcard or quiz session according to the material and check the set history.

## Expected result

The material is organized with its sources and study history.

## If something fails

If a question is incorrect, return to the source and correct the material. If generation finds no content, check document extraction and indexing.
