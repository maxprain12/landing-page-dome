Dome Companion controls **Desktop Many** from iPhone. After you sign in, Many is the main screen: there are no library tabs on the phone. You pair with a code the Mac generates in **Settings → Remote Many**. Documents stay on the desktop.

<Flow title="Remote Many">
  <FlowStep title="Code on the Mac">In Dome Desktop open **Settings → Remote Many** and generate the pairing code.</FlowStep>
  <FlowStep title="Pair on iPhone">Sign in, open **Account** (the avatar), and enter the code.</FlowStep>
  <FlowStep title="Mac awake">Leave the Mac awake with Desktop running. If it is off or asleep, the status is “Mac asleep”.</FlowStep>
  <FlowStep title="Open Many">In Companion, Many is the main screen. It is desktop Many, not a separate chat.</FlowStep>
  <FlowStep title="Send with context">Pick documents, skills, or MCP in **Context** (`+`) and send. Material travels through the tunnel (`refs.list` / `refs.preview`).</FlowStep>
  <FlowStep title="Approve in Draft">In Draft mode, mutations wait for your go-ahead. Sending email or publishing follows the same [human approval](/en/manual/email-and-social) rule.</FlowStep>
</Flow>

<Callout type="warning" title="If the Mac sleeps, Many is unavailable">
If the Mac is off or asleep, Many is unavailable. Companion does not open a cloud chat fallback.
</Callout>

<Callout type="note" title="The library does not upload to the cloud">
Documents, skills, and MCP are chosen in Context (`+`) and leave the Mac through the tunnel. Companion does not pull the library into the cloud.
</Callout>

## Context from the Mac

Open **Context** (`+`) and pick what Many should see. References travel with `refs.list` and `refs.preview`. `refs.export` brings the generated file to the iPhone to open it (Quick Look) if the format is visible on mobile. Typing `/` shows skill suggestions above the input.

The index and files stay on the Mac disk. If you have not imported material yet, go back to [organizing the library](/en/manual/library). The [PDF-to-follow-up](/en/blog/pdf-to-follow-up) loop is the same: document, person, draft, approval.

## Plan, Draft, and Agent

The **Plan**, **Draft**, and **Agent** modes travel with `mode.set` (and with the send). In Plan, Many asks with a questionnaire (one option, or “Write something”) and then Execute / Stay / Refine above the field. Draft asks for your ok to mutate. Nothing sensitive leaves without that go-ahead.

If this is your first time with Dome on the desktop, start with the [first workflow](/en/manual/first-workflow).
