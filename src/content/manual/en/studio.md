Prepare generated content and review results within the workspace.

## Requirements

- A provider and model supporting the generation type.
- Resources and permissions needed for the task.

## Steps

1. Open Studio or ask Many to prepare the content you need.
2. Provide sources, format and goal. Check the provider and requirements of the chosen tool.
3. Review generation status and open the result when available.
4. Check content, sources and format before exporting or using it.

## Expected result

The result appears in Dome for review and further work.

## If something fails

If generation fails, review the error, connection and model support. Not every modality is available from every provider.
