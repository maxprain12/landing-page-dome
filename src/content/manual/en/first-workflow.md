The primary product is a desktop app for macOS, Windows, and Linux. This guide covers the **Pro** loop: document, person, action. You do not need a cloud account to complete the trial.

1. **Download.** Open [GitHub Releases](https://github.com/maxprain12/dome/releases) and pick the installer for your system. Install and launch Dome.
2. **Choose Pro.** In onboarding, take the Pro edition. You can switch to Study or Dev later without deleting the library.
3. **Import a resource.** Add a note, PDF, video, audio file, or URL. The file stays in Dome’s local storage.
4. **Open or create a person.** In **People** (the contacts hub), relate the material to someone you need to follow up with.
5. **Ask for a draft.** Configure Ollama or your own keys. Open Many and ask for a brief, email, or note with the resource and person in view.
6. **Approve.** Review the text. Sending email or publishing to social waits for your go-ahead.

![Illustration of the people flow next to library material.](/how/how-people.png)

*The person’s history lives next to the resource that makes the conversation make sense.*

## What you can skip for now

You do not need to connect Gmail, social, or Provider to see whether Many understands a PDF. If you want the model local, read [Many with Ollama](/en/blog/many-with-ollama). If the resource is not Ready for AI, continue with [organizing the library](/en/manual/library).

> **An edition, not a paid plan.** Pro, Study, and Dev are focuses of the same app. They are not published plans. Cloud pricing is [TBA](/en/pricing).
