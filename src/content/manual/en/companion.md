Use Desktop Many from your iPhone.

## Requirements

- iPhone with iOS 17 or later.
- Dome on an awake Mac with remote Many enabled.
- A Dome account with Starter, Pro or Max.

## Steps

1. Check the Companion page for App Store or TestFlight when a published link is available.
2. In Dome for Mac, open Settings → Remote Many and enable remote control.
3. Generate a pairing code.
4. Sign in to Companion and open Account from the avatar to enter the code.
5. Return to chat and ask Many. Open references and review approval requests on mobile.

## Expected result

Companion controls Many on your Mac. Document and skill references travel through the tunnel; the phone does not need a full copy of the library.

## If something fails

If the Mac is off or asleep, remote Many is unavailable and there is no alternative cloud chat. Check your plan, connection and pairing. You can revoke the phone from Desktop.
