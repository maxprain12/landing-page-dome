The primary product is a desktop app for macOS, Windows, and Linux. This guide covers the **Pro** loop: document, person, action. You do not need a cloud account to complete the trial.

<Flow title="First workflow">
  <FlowStep title="Download">Open [GitHub Releases](https://github.com/maxprain12/dome/releases) and pick the installer for your system. Install and launch Dome.</FlowStep>
  <FlowStep title="Choose Pro">In onboarding, take the Pro edition. You can switch to Study or Dev later without deleting the library.</FlowStep>
  <FlowStep title="Import a resource">Add a note, PDF, video, audio file, or URL. The file stays in Dome’s local storage.</FlowStep>
  <FlowStep title="Open or create a person">In **People** (the contacts hub), relate the material to someone you need to follow up with.</FlowStep>
  <FlowStep title="Ask for a draft">Configure Ollama or your own keys. Open Many and ask for a brief, email, or note with the resource and person in view.</FlowStep>
  <FlowStep title="Approve">Review the text. Sending email or publishing to social waits for your go-ahead.</FlowStep>
</Flow>

## What you can skip for now

You do not need to connect Gmail, social, or Provider to see whether Many understands a PDF. If you want the model local, read [Many with Ollama](/en/blog/many-with-ollama). If the resource is not Ready for AI, continue with [organizing the library](/en/manual/library).

<Callout type="note" title="An edition, not a paid plan">
Pro, Study, and Dev are focuses of the same app. They are not published plans. Cloud pricing is [TBA](/en/pricing).
</Callout>
