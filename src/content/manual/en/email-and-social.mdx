Email and social are optional channels. Many can prepare the text from the library and the person record. **Send** and **publish** are human-in-the-loop actions: there is no background auto-send.

## Email

When the channel is connected, Many can read and search messages, then propose a send or a reply. That proposal waits for your ok. If you have not connected email, ask for the text anyway and copy it into your client.

## Social

The social hub groups accounts, content, and campaigns. A post draft publishes only after `social_post_publish` with your approval. Instagram requires **professional** accounts. Metrics are not a full history: some networks do not expose impressions or followers, and post import is capped.

<Callout type="warning" title="Real limits">
Do not treat the hub as agency analytics. Post import is partial. Member LinkedIn may not show followers. Check what the published Dome version exposes before promising a number.
</Callout>
