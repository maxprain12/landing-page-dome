Configure cloud services while keeping track of your local library.

## Requirements

- A Dome account with service access under its plan.
- Internet access for cloud services.

## Steps

1. Open Settings and review Dome sync.
2. Sign in and check your account access and sync status.
3. Review which data is synced and test with a test project before relying on the flow.
4. Configure remote Many separately for Companion; its references travel through the Desktop tunnel.

## Expected result

Settings show service status. Remote Many and sync are separate connections.

## If something fails

If sync fails, keep local data, check account and connection and review the error before retrying. Do not assume Companion contains a complete copy of your library.
